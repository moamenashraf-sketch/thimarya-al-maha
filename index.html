More than 60% of the project's area has been allocated to public facilities, services, and open spaces.

Commercial and Entertainment Malls

Main Avenue

The boulevard serves as the backbone of the Al Maha master plan; it is the central axis of the project that brings together all the structures to take visitors on a one-of-a-kind experience stretching over 4km and with a width of 150 meters in width and a total area estimated at approximately 6,000,000 square meters. It is situated between two 36-meter-wide streets and offers a full range of services: malls, restaurants, cafes, luxury stores, pharmacies, entertainment venues, parks, seating areas, and more… All are designed in a modern style featuring vast spaces, fluid movement, and seamless navigation between the blocks, in addition to dedicated parking for commercial, service, and entertainment areas.

4 km · 150 m wide · ~6,000,000 m²
Natural Areas and Valleys

Wadi al-Maha

The development is distinguished by its unique location on a plateau surrounded by wide valleys. A massive urban development is centered around streets ranging from 15 to 60 meters, with a full range of services: malls, restaurants, cafes, luxury stores, pharmacies, parks, and more… What further enhances residents' comfort is the track, a vast space for walking and an ideal place for physical activities, serving as a recreational area for children and adults alike.

Plateau with wide valleys · streets 15–60 m
Sports and Wellness Facilities

The Track

The track is more than just a place to run or walk; it is a comprehensive sports facility designed to promote health and well-being in our daily lives. In today's fast-paced world, where physical activity is becoming increasingly important, having such a vital facility is essential for encouraging people to adopt healthier lifestyles.
